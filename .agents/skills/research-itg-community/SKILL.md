# ITG Community Research

Investigate niche rhythm-game questions with broad discovery and claim-level evidence tracking. Treat community material as evidence with provenance, not as established fact.

## Load the research contract

1. In ThraxOS, read repository `AGENTS.md`, `memory/FACTS.md`, `memory/DECISIONS.md`, and `memory/PREFERENCES.md`.
2. Read [`references/evidence-rubric.md`](references/evidence-rubric.md) and [`references/output-format.md`](references/output-format.md) completely.
3. Read [`references/query-patterns.md`](references/query-patterns.md) before broad or difficult searches.
4. Read [`references/source-map.md`](references/source-map.md) when choosing communities or recovering historical material.
5. Use `$itg-packs-search` first for structured ITG Packs Release Spreadsheet questions. Use this skill to add reviews, history, reputation, chart-quality discussion, and other unstructured evidence.

## Frame the question

- Turn the request into explicit claims to test. Record the relevant product generation, software version, date range, play style, chart type, and desired confidence.
- Separate factual claims from recommendations and preferences.
- Identify what would disprove each important claim before searching.
- Treat hardware generations, firmware, software behavior, and community norms as time-sensitive.

## Search in layers

1. Search the exact phrase, product, pack, chart, author, error, or claim.
2. Expand with aliases, old names, abbreviations, usernames, spelling variants, version numbers, and date bounds.
3. For pack research, separate catalog metadata, release announcement, reputation evidence, gameplay preview, and archive provenance. One page rarely proves all five.
4. Search individual domains and communities; do not rely on one search engine's ranking or Reddit's internal search alone.
5. Follow summaries and reposts to the earliest accessible source. Prefer original developer statements, release notes, repositories, measurements, screenshots, and firsthand reports.
6. Use cached or archived copies when public pages are dead or materially changed. Never bypass authentication, access controls, robots restrictions, or private-community boundaries.
7. Search for counterevidence and failure reports after finding an apparent consensus.
8. Stop when the important claims are well supported, meaningfully disputed, or documented as unresolved. More low-quality repetition does not raise confidence.

Use web search for discovery and citations. Use an interactive browser only when a public page requires normal navigation or client-side rendering. Do not sign in, post, vote, message, or interact with a community unless the owner explicitly asks and authorizes that external action.

## Capture evidence

For every material claim, preserve:

- claim supported or contradicted;
- page title, author or handle, community, and direct URL;
- publication date and retrieval date;
- source type and whether the account is firsthand;
- short excerpt or precise paraphrase with enough surrounding context;
- applicable hardware generation, software version, and date range;
- corroboration, conflict, and confidence.

Do not infer community consensus from votes, search rank, one thread, repeated copies of the same claim, or missing disagreement. Do not identify deleted or pseudonymous users beyond what they publicly disclosed in the cited material.

## Synthesize conservatively

- Evaluate claims individually; one source may be strong for one claim and weak for another.
- Distinguish official fact, direct observation, firsthand report, community consensus, plausible inference, speculation, and unknown.
- Explain conflicts through version, hardware, environment, skill level, play style, or date when evidence supports that explanation.
- Keep advice scoped to the evidence. A Gen5 report does not automatically apply to Thraximundar's Gen4 stage; keyboard-chart opinions do not establish pad suitability.
- State what was searched but not found when absence matters. Absence of a result is not proof that something never existed.

## Respect ThraxOS boundaries

- Research is read-only. Do not download packs, change configuration, restart software, adjust pad settings, or mutate live systems through this skill.
- Never reproduce credentials, profile GUIDs, serial numbers, full USB instance IDs, cookies, private posts, or unnecessary personal and health data.
- Treat research findings as dated observations. Do not write them into repository memory unless the task includes a meaningful confirmed decision or the owner asks to preserve the findings.
- When action follows research, route it through `$thraxos`, `$add-pack`, or the relevant runbook and authorization boundary.

## Report

Lead with the answer and confidence. Provide a compact claim-evidence table for substantive investigations, followed by disagreements, applicability to Thraximundar, limitations, and next steps. Cite direct public pages near the claims they support.
