# Source map

Use this as a routing map, not a whitelist or a claim that every source is currently available. Discover and verify live locations during each task.

## Route by question

| Question | Start with | Then corroborate with |
|---|---|---|
| Software behavior or regression | Official repository, releases, issues, documentation | Maintainer posts, reproducible community reports |
| StepManiaX hardware or firmware | Official manuals, support material, release information | Generation-matched owner reports, photos, diagnostics, repair discussions |
| Pack identity, release, charts, or download | ITG Packs spreadsheet and publisher page | Author posts, song lists, reviews, archive snapshots |
| Chart quality or pad suitability | Chart files or videos, stepartist notes, experienced pad-player reports | Multiple independent players with comparable difficulty and play style |
| Historical community event or lineage | Contemporary announcement, tournament page, repository history | Archived forum threads, organizer or participant accounts |
| Reputation or consensus | Multiple threads across time and communities | Concrete examples, direct artifacts, counterexamples |
| GrooveStats behavior | Official GrooveStats material and integration code | Version-matched tournament or player reports |

## ThraxOS-approved pack discovery context

Read `docs/context/song-sources.md` for the current owner-approved structured pack sources and required recommendation metadata. Use `$itg-packs-search` for the live release spreadsheet. Community research supplements those sources; it does not replace archive validation or installation controls.

## Pack-discovery source roles

| Role | Preferred starting points | What they establish |
|---|---|---|
| Structured catalog | ITG Packs spreadsheet, ITGDb, ZIV simfiles, StepMania Online | Identity, searchable metadata, difficulty ranges, and candidate download links |
| Current community lead | r/StepMania release posts and monthly request thread; ZIV Simfile Discussion, Contests, and Requests | Recent releases, individual-song leads, author context, and community questions |
| Curated competitive feed | GrooveStats event pages, ITL, ECFA, Stamina RPG, and ITC recommendations | Tournament use, competitive relevance, and a stronger initial quality signal |
| Historical or descriptive reference | ITG Wiki pack pages and list; longstanding StepMania and ZIV forum threads | Lineage, dates, chart style, stepartists, revisions, and historical reception |
| Gameplay preview | Stepartist videos and established player playlists such as AlienSix | Visible chart behavior and musical fit, but not archive provenance or full-pack quality |
| Publisher evidence | Original author site, announcement, repository, or event download page | Exact version, declared compatibility, release notes, and the preferred archive source |

Do not collapse these roles. For example, Reddit can discover a release, a video can preview it, and ITGDb can describe it, but the original publisher or event page should establish the exact archive.

## Pack-source fit cautions

- StepMania Online is comprehensive but may mix pad and keyboard charts without reliable style labels. Corroborate chart type before recommending it for a stage.
- Etterna and Flash Flash Revolution are keyboard-first sources. File-format compatibility does not establish foot-playability.
- Newer Project OutFox Serenity content can depend on OutFox-specific modes, note types, audio formats, or behavior. Verify the exact edition against ITGMania rather than assuming shared-engine compatibility.
- GrooveStats and tournament packs are strong competitive signals but may skew above a household's difficulty range or toward tech, stamina, and unlock systems.
- Direct file-host links, reuploads, and personal mirrors need an original publisher trail, exact revision check, and the normal `$add-pack` safety workflow.
- Treat a current monthly request thread as a discovery venue, not evidence that a requested chart exists or will be produced.

## Community-source cautions

- Reddit search may omit old, deleted, or poorly indexed posts; supplement it with general web search and exact-title searches.
- Forum migrations can break permalinks, dates, attachments, and usernames. Verify quoted context and capture dates.
- Personal sites and file hosts may disappear or change ownership. Prefer archived copies and original publisher provenance.
- Video evidence can demonstrate behavior but may omit firmware, settings, modifications, or failed attempts.
- Chat screenshots and secondhand Discord quotations are weak unless the original speaker and context can be verified publicly.
- Wikis and FAQs can silently change; inspect revision history or archived versions when timing matters.

## Access boundaries

Use only public material or an authenticated session the owner explicitly asks to use. Do not evade bans, paywalls, login requirements, rate limits, robots restrictions, deleted-content controls, or private-community rules. Never contact a person as part of research without explicit authorization.
