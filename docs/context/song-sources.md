## Approved community discovery and screening sources

These sources supplement the structured catalogs. A recommendation, forum post, video, or event listing is a discovery lead, not by itself a verified archive source.

| Source | Best use | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| [r/StepMania](https://www.reddit.com/r/Stepmania/new/) | New pack announcements, current recommendations, and the monthly official simfile-request thread for individual songs | Community posts vary in quality; verify pad style, difficulty, sync, publisher, and archive provenance |
| [Zenius-I-vanisher Simulation Forums](https://zenius-i-vanisher.com/v5.2/forums/simfiles.php) | Simfile discussion, contests, requests, author announcements, and individual-song discovery | Mixed chart styles and creator experience levels |
| [GrooveStats](https://groovestats.com/) event pages | Current ranked, tournament, ITL, and Stamina RPG packs | Often competition-oriented and may require authentication; check difficulty and household fit |
| [International Timing Collective downloads](https://itc.dance/downloads/) | Curated examples of well-regarded pad-oriented tech and tech-stamina packs | A quality filter rather than a comprehensive release feed |
| [ITG Wiki pack list](https://itgwiki.dominick.cc/en/packs-and-simfiles/list-of-packs) | Pack history, lineage, release dates, difficulty ranges, and style context | Community-maintained reference; confirm current download and version details elsewhere |
| [StepMania Song Packs forum](https://www.stepmania.com/forums/song-packs/) | Long-running creator threads and occasional current releases | Low-volume and mixed keyboard/pad content; honor explicit style labels |
| [AlienSix YouTube playlists](https://www.youtube.com/@AlienSix/playlists) | Previewing music and charts from recent releases before deeper research | Preview evidence only; follow the pack back to its publisher or approved catalog |

For an individual song, search ZIV and StepMania Online first, then r/StepMania's current monthly request thread and the ZIV Simfile Requests forum. Do not post or request a chart without explicit owner authorization.

## Compatibility and quality cautions

- StepMania Online is broad and does not consistently distinguish pad, keyboard, tech, stamina, or gimmick content unless the uploader labels it.
- Etterna and Flash Flash Revolution are useful historical or keyboard-chart leads, but their content is keyboard-first and requires explicit evidence before treating it as suitable for a dance pad.
- Newer Project OutFox Serenity releases may use OutFox-specific modes, note types, audio, or engine behavior. Do not assume ITGMania compatibility from the shared StepMania lineage.
- Treat personal sites, social posts, and direct Google Drive or file-host links as provenance leads. Resolve the original publisher and exact release before downloading.
- Use videos to assess music, chart motion, and apparent pad play only. A video does not establish sync quality, complete difficulty coverage, archive safety, or ITGMania compatibility.

## Owner-confirmed taste model

Use the canonical family preference and chart model in [family-music-taste.md](family-music-taste.md). Clearly distinguish pad-oriented content from keyboard-oriented packs. Download only when explicitly requested or approved.

## Required metadata

For every recommendation, capture:

- pack and author;
- source page and direct archive source;
- release date or last update when available;
- pad/keyboard suitability and singles/doubles style;
- chart difficulty range and song count;
- archive size and checksum when available;
- overlap with installed packs or songs;
- reason it fits the current preference model.
