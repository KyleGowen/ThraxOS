- [ITGDb](https://itgdb.net/) combines and searches pack metadata sourced substantially from the ITG Packs spreadsheet and StepMania Online.

Community guidance recommends the ITG Packs spreadsheet as a primary pack index and emphasizes checking whether packs cover appropriate difficulty ranges. See [International Timing Collective's pack guide](https://itc.dance/2023/01/14/what-song-packs-should-i-download-for-itgmania/).

## Owner-confirmed taste model

Favor American pop, Top 100 EDM, 1980s music, alternative rock, and soundtracks. Exclude dubstep and anime-focused music. Recommendations should be suitable for real-pad play unless the owner says otherwise.

Difficulty range, singles/doubles preference, and desired session intensity should be gathered when material to a recommendation. Clearly distinguish pad-oriented content from keyboard-oriented packs. Download only when explicitly requested or approved.

## Required metadata

For every recommendation, capture:

- pack and author;
- source page and direct archive source;
- release date or last update when available;
- pad/keyboard suitability and singles/doubles style;
- chart difficulty range and song count;
- archive size and checksum when available;
- overlap with installed packs or songs;
- reason it fits the current preference model.
