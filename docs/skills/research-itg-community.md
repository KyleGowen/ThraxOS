# `research-itg-community` skill

Source: [`.agents/skills/research-itg-community/`](../../.agents/skills/research-itg-community/)

Read-only research for niche rhythm-game facts and history with per-claim provenance, applicability, counterevidence, and confidence.

## Components

- `references/evidence-rubric.md`, `output-format.md`, `query-patterns.md`, and `source-map.md`.
- Requires web search; an interactive browser is optional for public client-rendered pages.
- Uses `itg-packs-search` first for structured pack-index questions.

## Safety

Do not sign in, post, vote, message, bypass access controls, or mutate the host. Do not treat votes/reposts as consensus or reproduce private/identifying data.

## Reproduce and verify

1. Copy the skill and all references and enable web search.
2. Test a dated, version-scoped ITG question.
3. Confirm output separates official facts, firsthand reports, inference, conflicts, and unknowns with direct citations.
